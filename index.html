What we heard

Why we held these focus groups

At the Septemeber Town Hall General staff meeting, staff at OCDE were given the opportunity to sign-up for focus groups so they could give feedback on the 5-3-1 strategic plan. During sign-up, staff were able to select multiple areas they wanted to provide feedback on and they were invited to participate in at least one focus group from their preferred list.

The topic areas discussed at each focus group were based on the feedback you provided at Symposium #1 when you filled out a Google Form indicating what parts of your implementation plan you were open to feedback on.
